# Personalization Questions

**How to fill this in**
- Write after `A:`. Short answers are fine; details and examples are gold.
- Add a confidence tag at the end: **(K)** = I know for sure · **(L)** = likely · **(U)** = unsure/guess.
  "maybe / I think / idk" is fine, it just gets tagged (U) and I'll ask follow-ups.
- Skip anything you don't know. Leave it blank; I will never make things up.
- ⭐ = high impact. If you're short on time, answer these first.
- Inside jokes (Level 4 references) make the biggest emotional payoff. Be generous in §M.
